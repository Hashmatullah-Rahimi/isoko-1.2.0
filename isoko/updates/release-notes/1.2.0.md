# Isoko 1.2.0

**Date:** 2026-10-22
**Type:** Store update (new build) — adds a new native library (expo-location) and the location permission.

## What's new
- **Broker accounts.** A new "Broker" card on the welcome screen, next to Buyer & Renter and Owner (for both Create account and Log in). Brokers post and manage listings for their clients exactly like owners: posting, My listings, requests, messages, WhatsApp and Premium. Their listings show a purple "Broker" tag next to their name.
- **Search filters.** A filter button next to the search box (and on the Houses and Cars screens):
  - Sort: newest, lowest price, highest price
  - Price: minimum and maximum, in USD, EUR or RWF
  - Location: town or neighbourhood
  - Houses: bedrooms (1+ to 5+), bathrooms (1+ to 4+), property type, furnished, parking
  - Cars: year (from), fuel, gearbox
  - A small number on the button shows how many filters are on.
- **Premium listings (advertising).**
  - Owners and brokers: Profile → Premium, the gold "Premium" button in My listings, or on their own listing page.
  - They choose the listing and 1 week or 1 month, and send the request.
  - The Isoko team contacts them, takes the payment, and switches Premium on (see docs/08-BROKERS-PREMIUM-MAPS.md).
  - Premium listings show in a "Premium" row near the top of Home, come first in Houses, Cars and Search, and have a gold badge and frame.
  - Prices are in `src/config/app.ts` → `premium`.
- **Google Maps.**
  - When posting: "I'm here — use my location" pins the exact spot, or paste a Google Maps link.
  - On each listing: a map card with "Open map" (Google Maps) and "Directions".
  - Listings without a pin still open Google Maps at their written location.
