# Isoko 1.0.0

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Type:** Store update (first release)

## What's new
- The Isoko app for iPhone and Android.
- Three ways in: Buyer & Renter account, Owner account, or browse as a guest.
- Houses and Cars are fully separate, each with For sale, For rent and Accessories.
- Owners can post houses, cars and accessories with up to 10 photos.
- Buyers and renters can save listings and send buy/rent requests; owners accept or decline.
- The owner's phone number is only shared after they accept a request.

## Text for the App Store / Google Play "What's new"
> Welcome to Isoko — buy, rent and sell houses and cars, plus everything that goes with them.
